## Extracted Logic

Serenity's process is best modeled as a supply-chain Bayesian update loop:

1. Build a high-quality prior from industry structure, BOM, papers, customer maps, standards, capacity, and policy/capex flows.
2. Find the underpriced bottleneck one or two hops away from the obvious demand center.
3. Act before complete certainty when expected value is high and the thesis is falsifiable.
4. During holding, update confidence with primary and corroborating evidence.
5. Penalize substitution, customer insourcing, dilution, weak management, crowding, and route-change risk.
6. Rotate research and capital toward the currently highest-posterior bottleneck.
